<h2 class="font-headline text-3xl sm:text-4xl text-charcoal">
            Frequently Asked Questions by Parents
          </h2>
</div>
<div class="space-y-4">
<!-- FAQ 1 -->
<div class="bg-white p-6 rounded-2xl border border-[#f4cdd3]/80 shadow-xs">
<h3 class="font-headline text-xl text-charcoal font-semibold mb-2 flex items-center gap-2">
<span class="material-symbols-outlined text-[#ea8b99] text-[22px]">help_center</span>
              Is an echocardiogram painful or scary for my baby?
            </h3>
<p class="text-sm text-charcoal/75 leading-relaxed pl-8 font-light">
              Not at all. An echocardiogram uses safe ultrasound sound waves—there is zero radiation and zero pain. It feels like a gentle slide of a warm plastic probe on the chest with soft gel. Parents can hold their baby or give them milk during the scan.
            </p>
</div>
<!-- FAQ 2 -->
<div class="bg-white p-6 rounded-2xl border border-[#f4cdd3]/80 shadow-xs">
<h3 class="font-headline text-xl text-charcoal font-semibold mb-2 flex items-center gap-2">
<span class="material-symbols-outlined text-[#ea8b99] text-[22px]">help_center</span>
              Do I need a pediatrician's referral to schedule?
            </h3>
<p class="text-sm text-charcoal/75 leading-relaxed pl-8 font-light">
              While many parents are referred by their general pediatrician due to a murmur or symptoms, a formal referral letter is not strictly mandatory. You can reach out directly if you have specific family history, noticeable symptoms, or concerns about your child’s heart health.
            </p>
</div>
<!-- FAQ 3 -->
<div class="bg-white p-6 rounded-2xl border border-[#f4cdd3]/80 shadow-xs">
<h3 class="font-headline text-xl text-charcoal font-semibold mb-2 flex items-center gap-2">
<span class="material-symbols-outlined text-[#ea8b99] text-[22px]">help_center</span>
              How long does the entire evaluation take?
            </h3>
<p class="text-sm text-charcoal/75 leading-relaxed pl-8 font-light">
              We allocate 30 to 45 minutes for initial consultations. This provides adequate time for clinical examination, resting the child, performing the echocardiogram at an unhurried pace, and thoroughly discussing the findings with you.
            </p>
</div>
<!-- FAQ 4 -->
<div class="bg-white p-6 rounded-2xl border border-[#f4cdd3]/80 shadow-xs">
<h3 class="font-headline text-xl text-charcoal font-semibold mb-2 flex items-center gap-2">
<span class="material-symbols-outlined text-[#ea8b99] text-[22px]">help_center</span>
              How soon can we get an appointment?
            </h3>
<p class="text-sm text-charcoal/75 leading-relaxed pl-8 font-light">
              Routine appointments are typically booked within 24 to 48 hours. For newborns with suspected critical cyanotic defects, rapid breathing, or physician-flagged urgent cases, same-day priority review slots are reserved.
            </p>
</div>
</div>
