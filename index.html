A little wonder

A child’s heart beats faster than yours, and that’s perfectly normal.

Little hearts are small and busy, so they beat quicker. As children grow, their heartbeat slowly settles. Choose an age, then tap the heart to feel its rhythm.

Peace-of-mind guide

Preparing for your child’s visit

A heart clinic visit doesn’t have to be stressful. Here’s what to bring, what to say, and what to expect.

01 folder_shared

What to bring

Tick things off as you pack and we’ll remember for you.

Tip: feeding your baby just before the echo often helps them sleep right through it.
02 record_voice_over

How to talk to your child

  • Promise them there are no shots, injections or needles in an echo.
  • Tell them the doctor has a special camera and warm jelly to make a “movie” of their heartbeat.
  • Remind them you’ll be right beside them, holding their hand the whole time.
Kind, positive words turn the visit into a small adventure.
03 schedule

What to expect

  • An unhurried 30–45 minute consultation, just for your family.
  • A dim, quiet, cosy room with cartoons or soft nursery songs.
  • An honest conversation with Dr. Manvi, with diagrams and clear next steps.
No question is too small. Every one is welcome.
